# Case study content and provenance

Eight case studies use `src/case-studies.json` for expanded narratives and `src/projects.json` for original project metadata, visual assets, and design decisions.

Each narrative includes an executive summary, discovery frame, problem statement, synthesis, representative journey, scope, information architecture and wireframe exploration priorities, design tradeoffs, proposed validation scenarios, delivery requirements, and reflection.

## Evidence boundaries

- Existing repository images and project themes provide the starting context. Their presence is not verification of historical results.
- The eight `assets/process/*-board.png` images are AI-generated reconstructions for presentation. They are explicitly labeled illustrative synthesis and are not original FigJam exports or editable FigJam files.
- Research methods are reconstructed framing; no new interviews, participant quotations, sample sizes, or measured outcomes are asserted.
- Validation scenarios describe proposed evaluation, not completed studies.
- Wireframe sections describe exploration priorities, not recovered historical wireframes.
- Confidential internal company materials are not reproduced. No new employer attribution was assigned to projects.
- Before public presentation, the portfolio owner should align the role, scope, and reconstructed decision narrative with what they can personally explain. Replace planned evaluation with actual findings only when verified evidence is available.

## Updating the content

Keep the distinction between a design hypothesis, a reconstructed artifact, and an observed outcome. Add actual constraints, collaboration details, and decisions from recollection where accurate; do not add precision merely to make the story sound more credible.
