A configurable analytics canvas that helps teams move from a high-level signal to the data underneath.

Problem

Analytics work was split across too many surfaces.

Dashboards summarized the signal, chart builders handled exploration, grids held detail, and sharing or export lived elsewhere. The experience made it hard to keep context while moving from “what changed?” to “why?” and “what next?”

My contribution

I designed the canvas model and built the working experience.

I mapped the analytical jobs, shaped the information architecture, defined the component and state model, and translated the system into a runnable React and TypeScript prototype.

Design response

Keep overview, configuration, and evidence connected.

  • Composable widgets with visible page structure
  • Progressive settings for data, display, filters, and advanced rules
  • Charts and grids treated as equal analytical views
  • Commentary, saved views, themes, and export in the same workspace

Outcome

A complete analytics loop people can test.

The prototype supports scanning, configuring, exploring, explaining, saving, and exporting with deterministic demo data and clear seams for authenticated data, AI, quality, and persistence services.
